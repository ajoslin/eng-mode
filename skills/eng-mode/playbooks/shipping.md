### Shipping

**Shipping alone lands, and only with explicit merge authority.** Babysit establishes merge-readiness but never merges or arms merge-when-ready.

1. Resolve the selected provider and require its documented operations for PR snapshot, watcher events and rearm, topology, arming confirmation, merge-when-ready request and disarm, and current-head/base comparison. If any operation is missing, stop; never fall back to another provider. Snapshot every queued PR's base, head SHA, checks, threads, merge state, and provider-owned parentage. Name the provider's topology owner and freeze the bottom-to-top queue and parentage before verification.
2. For every meaningful-risk PR, commission three independently produced, authorship-independent lanes against the same head SHA and base: **repository gates** from `project-standards`; **live real-surface proof** through `verify-project` or the other mapped actual surface; and **receipts-plus-diff audit**, which inspects the base-to-head diff and the other lanes' durable evidence. Each lane returns `PASS`, `PASS+NOTES`, or `FAIL` pinned to that SHA. A command-only change may instead use the producer's exact command receipt plus an independent coordinator spot-check; record that exception rather than creating ceremonial agents.
3. `VERIFIED(<sha>)` is the aggregate only when all three required lanes pass (`PASS` or `PASS+NOTES`) for that exact SHA and the aggregate verdict, lane producers, and evidence pointers are posted on its PR. `FAIL`, missing evidence, or `INCONCLUSIVE` does not pass. A new head SHA immediately voids the verdict unless current-head/base comparison and `git patch-id` prove the old and new patches identical; post that identity proof and repin the verdict to the new SHA before proceeding. Stale SHA evidence never passes silently.
4. `STACK-READY(<sha>)` means that exact SHA is `VERIFIED`, Babysit reports it merge-ready with its review-agent loop complete, the SHA-pinned verdict is posted on its PR, and the named topology owner has safely appended it through the provider's topology operation. The **ceiling** is the highest PR in the uninterrupted bottom-to-top run of `STACK-READY` heads. Stop at the first gap; nothing above it is landable. Extending or moving the ceiling requires a new verification pass against the candidate's current SHA and base, not reuse of an earlier ceiling decision.
5. Re-snapshot the contiguous run immediately before arming. Any changed head, base, topology, checks, approvals, merge state, or review-agent state invalidates the affected readiness and requires the corresponding new pass. A PR remains blocked while Babysit's review-agent loop is unfinished or parked `review-agent-unavailable`; green CI alone is not that loop.
6. Land only the frozen contiguous run through the selected provider. For each PR, issue the provider's merge-when-ready request, then observe its documented arming-confirmation state for that exact PR and current head; a successful request, proxy field, or assumed auto-merge state is not proof. If previously armed through the wrong mechanism, use the selected provider's documented disarm operation before continuing. Record the confirmation evidence.
7. The **drain** begins at the first confirmed arm and ends when the frozen run lands or Shipping stops at a blocker. During it, preserve non-interference: no sync, restack, submit-stack, force-push, parentage change, queue append, or other topology mutation; re-parent independent new work onto trunk rather than the draining chain. Diagnose a stall from provider state before mutating anything. If mutation becomes necessary, stop the drain, disarm safely, void affected readiness, freeze a new snapshot, and reverify.
8. Drive the provider watcher using its documented event schema. Interpret `READY` as eligible to arm or continue, `WAITING` as nonterminal with a named unmet gate, `ADVANCE` as a landed frontier that requires re-snapshotting the next PR rather than declaring completion, and `COMPLETE` as the frozen run fully landed. After every nonterminal event, new head, or reconnect, rearm the watcher from its documented cursor/state and confirm the watched PR and head; unknown or dropped events stop the drain. Prefer the blocking wait. If repeated turns are intrinsic, the main session keeps the landing outcome in `goal` and invokes `loop` with a bounded prompt; stop the loop at `COMPLETE` or a blocker.
9. Report selected provider; frozen queue and topology owner; each arm proof and its producer; verification producers, SHA-pinned lane verdicts, and posted aggregate; ceiling and every pass used to establish or extend it; watcher events/rearms; landed PRs; review-agent quiet SHA or `review-agent-unavailable`; drain start/end and any interference; and the first gap or `COMPLETE`.
